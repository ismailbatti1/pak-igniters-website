# PAK IGNITERS: WooCommerce Store Specification
**Lead:** Wardah (WordPress & WooCommerce Lead)  

---

## 1. Product Catalog Architecture

The *Pak Igniters Commerce* division will service 3 product types:

1. **Academy Course Enrollments (Virtual / Simple Product):**
   - Full-Stack Web Development Bootcamp
   - WordPress & WooCommerce Mastery
   - AI Tools & Digital Automation
2. **Digital Services & Packages (Variable Products):**
   - Web Studio Starter, Growth, and Enterprise web design packages
   - SEO & Performance marketing retainers (Pak Igniters Digital)
3. **Business Consulting (Bookings / Services):**
   - Corporate Digital Advisory sessions (Pak Igniters Business Solutions)

---

## 2. Payment Gateway Matrix (Pakistan & International)

| Gateway | Supported Currencies | Target Audience |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **PayFast / JazzCash / EasyPaisa** | PKR | Local Pakistani students & businesses |
| **Stripe / PayPal** | USD / EUR / GBP | Overseas Pakistani & International clients |
| **Direct Bank Transfer (IBAN)** | PKR | High-ticket enterprise consulting |

---

## 3. Security & Checkout Flow

- SSL enforced on all checkout endpoints.
- Guest checkout enabled for single masterclass registrations.
- Customer account dashboard for Academy students to access learning materials.
